Root cause and contributing factors
process
Ravi Menon closed ORB-101 as Resolved in 2.1.3 on internal-only verification ('totals verified on our side') without reproducing the client's finance sandbox export path; Maya Rao reopened it five days later because the sandbox still showed blank VAT totals on 2.1.3, showing the verification was premature and mis-scoped.
technical
Maya Rao identified on ORB-150 that the BILLING-V2 refactor moved VAT rounding to integer math in 2.1.0, dropping fractional cents and blanking the export totals, and that the change was still unreviewed — i.e. the defect was a separate rounding root cause that the 2.1.3 release did not address.
process
Maya Rao recorded the VAT fix as delivered in 2.1.4, with the VAT rounding path corrected and exports verified with finance — the actual delivery milestone for the blank-totals defect, after the Sep 20/2.1.3 commitment failed.
technical
The VAT blank-totals defect was caused by the BILLING-V2 refactor moving VAT rounding to integer math in 2.1.0, which drops fractional cents and blanks the export totals; that change was never reviewed (ORB-150).
technical
Contoso reported that VAT invoice exports showed blank totals since the 2.1.0 upgrade, blocking finance reconciliation — the client-visible symptom of the rounding regression.
technical
VAT blank-totals root cause: the BILLING-V2 refactor moved VAT rounding to integer math in 2.1.0, dropping fractional cents and blanking export totals, and that change remains unreviewed (ORB-150).
technical
The VAT rounding regression had its own tracking ticket, ORB-150 (BILLING-V2: VAT rounding regression), opened by Ravi Menon on 2024-09-10 — separate from client ticket ORB-101 and never linked into the 2.1.3 resolution.
process
Ravi Menon resolved ORB-101 on 2024-09-21 stating 'totals verified on our side' for 2.1.3 — an internal-only verification, with no record of reproducing the client's finance sandbox export path.
process
Ravi Menon announced the ORB-101 closure on the support channel as 'the VAT fix is in 2.1.3', propagating the premature resolution to the client-facing channel.
technical
Maya Rao reopened ORB-101 on 2024-09-26 because the client's finance sandbox still showed blank VAT totals on 2.1.3, confirming the Sep 20/2.1.3 fix did not resolve the defect.
process
The reopen was also announced on orbit-support by Maya Rao on 2024-09-26, publicly correcting the earlier 'fixed in 2.1.3' claim.
technical
The client's original report (Dana Okafor, 2024-09-02) ties the blank totals to the 2.1.0 upgrade and states finance is blocked on reconciliation.
technical
Dana Okafor logged ORB-101 on 2024-09-02 as VAT invoice exports showing blank totals after the 2.1.0 upgrade, with finance blocked on reconciliation — the client-visible symptom that the 2.1.3 fix did not clear.
process
